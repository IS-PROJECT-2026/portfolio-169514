Ayana Ndete

I build things at the intersection of IoT and AI — systems where sensors, microcontrollers, and language models all have to work together in the real world, not just in a demo.

My RAG capstone project explores how retrieval-augmented generation can make AI responses more grounded and accurate by pulling from real data sources instead of relying purely on a model's memory. On the hardware side, my Smart Farm system uses an ESP32 to monitor soil and environmental conditions, giving small-scale farmers real-time data to make better decisions. I've also built a security system that combines sensor input with automated alerts — practical engineering, not just proof-of-concept.

What ties it together: I like systems that sense the world, reason about it, and act on it.
